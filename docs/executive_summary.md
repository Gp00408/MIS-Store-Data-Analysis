# Executive Summary

**Executive Summary**

Based on the sales and profit analysis over the past 48 months, December 2017 sales growth came in at -29.23% month-over-month and -13.58% year-over-year. This downward trend points to a sustained decline in sales and suggests the time has come for a strategic response from management. In particular, the recent sales decline may reflect changes in market conditions as well as internal operational efficiency issues.

The outlier analysis identified a sales anomaly in November 2017, which can be interpreted as the effect of a specific promotion or marketing campaign. Profit anomalies, on the other hand, occurred in January 2015, October 2016, December 2016, and March 2017, indicating cost-structure changes or unusual transactions during those periods. These anomalies can serve as important reference points for forecasting future sales and profit.

An analysis of the top 3 highest-loss orders found that all of them involved transactions with high discount rates. In particular, order CA-2016-108196 recorded a loss of -6599.98, underscoring the negative impact discounting can have on profitability. This pattern points to the need to revisit pricing and discount strategy going forward.

The actionable insights proposed to management are, first, to develop an effective marketing strategy to overcome the sales decline, and second, to adjust discount policy to minimize losses. Together, these steps create an opportunity to improve customer acquisition and profitability at the same time.
